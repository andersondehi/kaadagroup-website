    From the field to the buyer

    We know the field before we write the first line of code.

    Traceability, compliance with the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR), producer registries and payments, child protection: we design tools that start from the real requirements of African value chains.

    1. 01 · Field

      Survey every plot, even offline

      The field officer traces the polygon by GPS on the phone and registers the farmer. Data is sent as soon as the network is back.

    2. 02 · Checks

      Check and correct automatically

      Overlaps, self-intersections, area gaps: every polygon goes through control rules, and simple errors are fixed without re-entry.

    3. 03 · Satellite

      Assess deforestation risk

      Each plot is compared with forest cover on 31 December 2020 and with satellite alerts, right inside the application.

    4. 04 · Export

      Hand over an export-ready file

      Lots traced back to the plot, risk assessment and a complete due diligence statement, ready to submit in TRACES.
