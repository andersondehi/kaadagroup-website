    Sector

    Waste collection and recycling: trace material flows and pay collectors.

    Recyclers and brands need to know where the materials they buy come from and under what conditions they were collected. We design, build and deploy the tools that record every collector, every weighing and every payment.

    Start a project See our approach
    The same foundations as agritech

    Identify every actor, record every transaction, track every lot.

    A collector bringing materials to a buy-back centre and a producer delivering a harvest to a cooperative raise the same questions: who, how much, at what price, paid how. We apply to recycling the traceability methods we develop for agricultural value chains. Producers and cooperatives

    What we design

    From the collector to the recycler.

    01

    Collector registration

    A collector registry, with a unique ID and the information needed for payment.

    02

    Weighing at buy-back centres

    Weight, material type and price recorded at every drop-off, even with no network.

    03

    Mobile money payment

    Every collector paid by mobile money, with an SMS receipt linked to their weighing.

    04

    Material flow traceability

    Lots of material tracked from the buy-back centre to the recycler, with volumes at each stage.
