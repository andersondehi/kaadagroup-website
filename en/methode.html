    Approach

    How we roll out your tools in the field.

    Software is only useful if it is used. Our approach starts from your teams and your timeline, and goes all the way to support after go-live.

    Start a project
    The steps

    Six steps, approved with you one by one.

    Each step ends with your approval. Nothing moves forward without your agreement.

    1. 01

      Assessment

      A 45-minute conversation, by video call or in Abidjan, about your objectives, your existing data and your field constraints.

      Free
    2. 02

      Scoping and design with users

      The scope set with you, mock-ups tested with field agents and managers, then development delivered in stages.

      Approved with you
    3. 03

      Pilot in one area

      A first, limited rollout to test the tool in real conditions and adjust it.

      Adjusted
    4. 04

      Training for field agents and cooperatives

      In the field, on the agents’ own phones. We also train in-house champions in your organisation.

      In the field
    5. 05

      Rollout aligned with the season

      Go-live scheduled before the season opens, not during peak purchasing.

      Scheduled
    6. 06

      Support and maintenance

      Monitoring, user support and enhancements after go-live.

      Service agreement

    All our expertise

    Realities in the field

    What we plan for from the design stage.

    A tool designed in the office has to work in the field. We start from real conditions of use.

    The network as it really is

    Applications that work offline and synchronise as soon as the network returns, with lightweight data.

    Local payment

    Mobile money integration for purchases, premiums and field agents’ pay.

    Field teams

    Enumerators and mappers recruited, trained and supervised for each assignment.

    Daily quality control

    Duplicates, inconsistent polygons and outliers flagged every day, during data collection.

    The season calendar

    Training and go-lives scheduled outside peak purchasing periods.

    What you already have

    Migration and cleaning of your registries and maps, even when they are spread across several providers.

    Our agritech domains

    Your data

    Your data belongs to you.

    The data collected is yours. It can be exported at any time in open formats, and you do not depend on any vendor to use it.

    Formats

    GeoJSON and Shapefile for maps, CSV for tables, APIs for your other systems.

    Frequently asked questions

    Your questions about rollout.

    How long does a project take?
    It depends on the scope. We set the timeline with you after the assessment, taking your season dates into account.
    Who trains our field agents?
    Our team, in the field. We also train in-house champions in your organisation, so that new agents can be trained internally.
    What happens after go-live?
    We provide maintenance and support under a service agreement defined with you.

    A tool to roll out before your next season?

    Let’s talk about your timeline and your teams. Free assessment: a 45-minute conversation, by video call or in Abidjan.
