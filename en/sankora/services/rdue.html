    Traceability and compliance

    EUDR compliance

    Know before you ship whether your plots meet the EUDR.

    Each plot is compared with forest cover on 31 December 2020, using satellite data and your own layers. The results feed an Article 10 risk assessment, approved by a second person.

    Talk to an expert How it works
    Why it matters

    What the regulation changes for you

    01

    Deadlines are close

    Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 applies from 30 December 2026 to large and medium-sized companies, and from 30 June 2027 to micro and small enterprises. Your European buyers are already asking for proof.

    02

    No deforestation after 2020

    A product can only enter the EU market if its plots were not deforested after 31 December 2020. A single doubtful plot can make a whole lot unsellable in Europe.

    03

    Classified forests and protected areas

    A plot that encroaches on a classified forest or protected area also raises a legality issue. You need to catch it before purchase, not after loading.

    04

    One-off studies age quickly

    An analysis commissioned once from a third party covers neither new plots nor recent alerts. Evidence must be kept up to date and on file.

    How it works

    How Sankora assesses your plots

    1. 1

      Every plot analysed

      The polygon in force, or failing that the GPS point with a 50-metre buffer, is compared with 2020 forest cover, year-by-year tree cover loss and recent alerts. Global Forest Watch data and your own layers are combined, and the least favourable source prevails.

    2. 2

      A clear status, evidence kept

      Each plot gets a status: deforestation-free, to be checked, likely deforestation or analysis not possible. The analysis is stored with its sources, their versions and a SHA-256 fingerprint.

    3. 3

      A reasoned human decision

      A satellite result is a signal, not a verdict. An authorised person clears or confirms each flag, with a reason, a type of evidence and a document.

    4. 4

      Risk assessment

      For a lot, container, shipment, contract or supplier, Sankora computes the measurable criteria: geolocation, polygons, deforestation, protected areas, mixing, duplicates. You fill in the legality criteria and mitigation measures.

    5. 5

      Four-eyes approval

      Whoever drafted or contributed to the assessment cannot approve it. A "negligible" conclusion is only possible if all residual levels are low, and the list of assessed plots is then frozen.

    6. 6

      Shipments checked

      An EUDR lot containing a plot with likely deforestation is not compliant. An assessment only covers a shipment if all its plots are within its scope.

    Frequently asked questions

    Your questions about this service.

    Is a satellite result enough as proof?

    No. It is a reasoned signal that guides verification: the reference forest map sometimes classifies agroforestry cocoa as forest. That is why an authorised person clears or confirms each flag, with evidence.

    Can a plot without a polygon be analysed?

    Yes, if it is 4 hectares or less: its GPS point is analysed with a 50-metre buffer. Above 4 hectares, the regulation requires a polygon.

    Is Rainforest Alliance or Fairtrade certification enough?

    No. In Sankora, a certification does not equal EUDR compliance. It is taken into account as complementary information in the risk assessment.

    Who decides that a risk is negligible?

    Your organisation, following a logged method. Sankora computes what it can, requires a second person to approve, and refuses a "negligible" conclusion while any residual level remains medium or high.
