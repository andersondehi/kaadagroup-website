    Traceability and compliance

    Farmer and plot register

    Know every farmer and every plot, with the evidence to show it.

    The register is the foundation of traceability: a purchase, a lot or an EUDR statement only holds if the plot of origin is known and properly geolocated. Sankora takes over your existing files, checks every GPS survey and tells you exactly what still needs fixing.

    Talk to an expert How it works
    Why it matters

    Why a reliable register changes everything

    01

    The EUDR requires geolocation

    Every product sold in the European Union must be linked to the plots where it was produced. A GPS point is enough up to 4 hectares; beyond that, a polygon is required. Without a geolocated register, no statement is possible.

    02

    Field surveys are often flawed

    Unclosed polygons, GPS spikes, overlaps, swapped coordinates: surveys from several providers contain errors. When the buyer finds them, whole lots are held up.

    03

    Duplicates distort volumes

    A farmer registered twice can deliver more than their plots produce. These gaps undermine the whole organisation's credibility with its buyers.

    04

    History must stay accurate

    A plot changes hands, a polygon gets corrected. Years later, you must still be able to show the exact situation on the day of each purchase.

    How it works

    How it works

    1. 1

      Bring in your register

      You fill in Sankora's official Excel template with your farmers and plots. Every row is checked in a preview, then the import runs in one go, all or nothing.

    2. 2

      Upload GPS surveys

      Your field officers or providers record plot points and boundaries in the field. You upload their GeoJSON, KML, KMZ, GPX or ZIP files, whatever tool they used.

    3. 3

      Rule-by-rule checks

      Each polygon is checked for closure, spikes, self-intersections, overlaps, area, country, classified forests and protected areas. Minor defects are fixed automatically and the original survey is kept.

    4. 4

      Fix it at the source

      Major defects are explained, in French and English, so the field team can redo the survey. A rejected polygon can be quarantined: it leaves maps and statistics without being lost.

    5. 5

      A register ready for what comes next

      Each plot carries its EUDR geolocation status and its estimated output. Purchases, lots and EUDR files build directly on this register.

    Frequently asked questions

    Frequently asked questions.

    What tool should we use to survey plots?

    Whatever your field officers or providers already use: GPS unit, phone or mapping app. Sankora reads the resulting files and applies the same checks to every survey, whatever its source.

    Can Sankora fill in a plot with no GPS point?

    No. Sankora never generates a point or a polygon: a made-up coordinate would create a non-compliance. The plot stays flagged as not geolocated until a field survey is provided.

    What happens to a corrected polygon?

    Each upload creates a version. The original survey is kept alongside the corrected version, and a rejected version never replaces the compliant one in force.

    How are duplicates handled?

    Sankora alerts without blocking. You decide: two different people, or the same person, with the records merged if you wish; the record you keep then takes over the other's plots.
