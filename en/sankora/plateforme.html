    One register, the whole chain

    Four layers, one source of truth.

    Every module reads and enriches the same register of farmers and plots. Start with the core and add the modules you need, at your organisation's pace.

    1. 01RegisterFarmers, plots and polygons in one reference base, checked automatically.
    2. 02FlowsPurchases, payments, lots, shipments and receipts, linked to their plots of origin.
    3. 03EUDR complianceDeforestation analysis, risk assessment and due diligence file.
    4. 04MonitoringSeason indicators, automatic alerts and an assistant for your summaries.
    Layer 1 · The core

    A clean register, verified polygons.

    Sankora takes your existing Excel files and GPS surveys, then checks every plot before accepting it. Common defects are corrected automatically; the others are explained, rule by rule, so they can be fixed at the source.

    • Import of the Excel register and GeoJSON, KML or GPX polygons
    • Reasoned diagnosis: unclosed polygon, GPS spike, overlap, swapped coordinates, inconsistent area
    • Logged automatic corrections, with the original survey kept
    • EUDR 4-hectare rule: a GPS point is enough up to 4 ha, a polygon is required above

    Layer 2 · Flows

    Every kilo linked to its plot, from purchase to container.

    From the farm-gate weighing ticket to receipt at the exporter, every movement is recorded and linked to its plots of origin, within their estimated production.

    • Purchases with weighing ticket, tare per bag, moisture and season price
    • Cap per plot: volumes above estimated production are flagged as over the cap
    • Lots EUDR-compliant only when 100% traced, no mass balance
    • Waybill read automatically, lot file sent to the exporter with its digital fingerprint
    • Traced payments to farmers: cash, mobile money, transfer or cheque

    Layer 3 · Compliance

    Deforestation analysis, right inside the system.

    Every plot is compared with the forest situation on 31 December 2020, using satellite data and your own layers. Results, assessments and decisions are kept as evidence and assembled into an exportable file.

    • Automatic analysis using Global Forest Watch data: 2020 tree cover, annual losses, recent alerts
    • A clear status per plot: deforestation-free, to review, probable deforestation
    • Risk assessment and mitigation measures, in line with Articles 10 and 11 of the regulation
    • File per shipment: statement data, geolocation in the format expected by the EU, PDF report
    • Four-eyes rule: the person who drafts is never the one who approves

    Frequently asked questions

    What we are asked most often.

    Our polygons come from several providers: is that a problem?
    No. Sankora applies the same checks to every survey, whatever its source or format. Common defects are corrected automatically; the others are listed with their reason so the provider can fix them.
    Can we bring in our existing data?
    Yes. Sankora imports your farmer and plot register from Excel, and your polygons in GeoJSON, KML or GPX. Every line is checked before import and errors are explained.
    How does Sankora analyse deforestation risk?
    Each plot (its polygon, or its GPS point with a buffer zone) is compared with tree cover on 31 December 2020, year-by-year tree cover loss and recent alerts, using Global Forest Watch data and the layers you import. The least favourable source prevails, and every analysis is kept as evidence.
    Does Sankora file the due diligence statement?
    Sankora prepares the complete file: statement data, geolocation in the expected format, analyses, risk assessment and supporting documents. Filing in the EU information system remains the responsibility of the operator placing the product on the EU market.
    Which commodities are covered?
    Ivorian cocoa first. Sankora is designed for several commodities (coffee, cashew, rubber…) and several countries: products, seasons, prices and rules are configured per commodity.
    How is Sankora priced?
    By subscription: a set-up fee, then a monthly fee based on the modules chosen and the number of farmers. The quote is prepared after the demo.
