EU Deforestation Regulation

The EUDR is coming. Sankora gets you ready.

Cocoa, coffee, rubber and their derived products sold in the European Union will have to come from deforestation-free, geolocated plots covered by a due diligence statement.

  1. 31/12/2020

    Cut-off date

    No plot may have been cleared after this date.

  2. 30/12/2026

    Large and medium companies

    The regulation applies to medium and large operators and traders.

  3. 30/06/2027

    Micro and small companies

    Application to the smallest operators.

Frequently asked questions

Who must submit a due diligence statement?

The operator who places the product on the EU market for the first time. Downstream operators and traders keep information on their suppliers and customers for five years.

Is a GPS point or a polygon needed?

A GPS point is enough for a plot of 4 hectares or less. Above that, the full boundary of the plot is required.

What does Sankora do when a plot has a problem?

The plot is quarantined and sent back for correction. Sankora never generates coordinates: everything in a file was recorded in the field.

Do all my cooperatives have to use Sankora?

No. An exporter can receive data from partner cooperatives in Sankora, and each cooperative decides what it shares.
