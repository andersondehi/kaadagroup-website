    Sankora in screenshots

    From plot to export file, in four steps.

    This is Sankora for real: every screen is a capture of the platform, with demo data.

    1. 01 · Field

      Survey every plot, even offline

      The field officer traces the polygon by GPS on the phone and registers the farmer. Data is sent as soon as the network is back.

    2. 02 · Checks

      Check and correct automatically

      Overlaps, self-intersections, area gaps: every polygon goes through control rules, and simple errors are fixed without re-entry.

    3. 03 · Satellite

      Assess deforestation risk

      Each plot is compared with forest cover on 31 December 2020 and with satellite alerts, right inside the application.

    4. 04 · Export

      Hand over an export-ready file

      Lots traced back to the plot, risk assessment and a complete due diligence statement, ready to submit in TRACES.

    EU Deforestation Regulation

    The EUDR is coming. Sankora gets you ready.

    Cocoa, coffee, rubber, oil palm, soy and their derived products sold in the European Union will have to come from deforestation-free, geolocated plots covered by a due diligence statement.

    1. 31/12/2020

      Cut-off date

      No plot may have been cleared after this date.

    2. 30/12/2026

      Large and medium companies

      The regulation applies to medium and large operators and traders.

    3. 30/06/2027

      Micro and small companies

      Application to the smallest operators.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who must submit a due diligence statement?

    The operator who places the product on the EU market for the first time. Downstream operators and traders keep information on their suppliers and customers for five years.

    Is a GPS point or a polygon needed?

    A GPS point is enough for a plot of 4 hectares or less. Above that, the full boundary of the plot is required.

    What does Sankora do when a plot has a problem?

    The plot is quarantined and sent back for correction. Sankora never generates coordinates: everything in a file was recorded in the field.

    Do all my cooperatives have to use Sankora?

    No. An exporter can receive data from partner cooperatives in Sankora, and each cooperative decides what it shares.
