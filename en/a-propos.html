    About

    An African technology company, grounded in the field.

    Kaada Group (KDG) is a technology company based in Abidjan. Our ambition: to accelerate digital transformation in Africa. We support businesses, institutions and organisations from defining their digital strategy through to the design, development, integration and rollout of solutions tailored to their needs.

    Meet the team Contact us
    Who we are

    Technology expertise informed by international practice, applied to African realities.

    We work in digital transformation consulting, software and web and mobile application development, information systems, cloud, artificial intelligence, process automation and making the most of data.

    Agriculture holds a strategic place in our approach. For our clients, we design solutions that improve operations management, traceability, the use of data and decision-making in agricultural value chains. It is our speciality, not our limit.

    Our strength lies in bringing three things together: technology, knowledge of agricultural issues and an understanding of African economic and operational realities, from intermittent connectivity to mobile money payments. Our ambition is to make digital technology a practical lever for performance, competitiveness and progress in Africa.

    Our mission

    To design, integrate and roll out technology solutions that address the challenges of African organisations.

    To improve their performance and enable them to take full advantage of digital technology, data and artificial intelligence. Our role: to bring what modern technology makes possible closer to the real needs of businesses and organisations across the continent.

    Our vision

    A more innovative, more productive and more competitive Africa.

    We want to help build an Africa where digital technology accelerates business growth and the modernisation of economic sectors. We are convinced that digital technology, data and artificial intelligence can accelerate the continent’s economic transformation.

    Kaada“to bring together” in Guro, a language of Côte d'Ivoire

    Our name states our mission: to bring together talent, ideas and technology for African progress. Our symbol represents Africa as a connected network: the points are the people, organisations and opportunities; the links are collaboration, innovation and synergy.

    Useful innovation

    Technology that solves real problems and creates measurable value.

    Excellence

    Quality, reliability, security and continuous improvement, from design to rollout.

    Closeness

    Understanding your real needs and building with you solutions that fit your objectives, your resources and your constraints.

    Openness

    International expertise, knowledge sharing and African skills, brought together for relevant solutions.

    Lasting impact

    Technology that improves the performance of organisations over the long term and supports African economic development.

    Our value proposition

    Three strengths, under one partner.

    An IT services provider brings the technology; a consulting firm brings knowledge of the field. We bring both together, with a specialisation in agriculture.

    Technology expertise informed by international practice

    We draw on proven skills, methods and practices to design robust, scalable solutions tailored to your needs and constraints.

    Agricultural sector expertise

    Producers and cooperatives, traceability, regulatory compliance, mapping, geospatial data and decision support: we know agricultural value chains and what they require.

    An understanding of African realities

    Connectivity, infrastructure, user skills, budgets and local practices: we design for real-world conditions of use. The right technology, an understanding of the business and the realities of the field.

    Our way of working

    How we work in Africa.

    Our advantage is not a particular technology. It is knowing the conditions in which your tools will be used.

    The cooperative as the client

    We design tools that the cooperative uses itself, not just data collection tools for the buyer’s benefit.

    The network as it really is

    Offline first, synchronisation as soon as the network is back, lightweight data.

    Local payments

    Mobile money integration to pay producers, collectors and agents.

    The value chain calendar

    Rollouts planned before the season opens, and training outside peak buying periods.

    Building on what exists

    Cleaning and merging registries and maps scattered across several providers.

    Your data belongs to you

    Exports in open formats and no vendor lock-in.
