    Data4ChildA Sankora module · Kaada Group

    Every child identified, followed until they exit.

    Data4Child is Sankora's child labour monitoring and remediation system (CLMRS). Your agents collect data in the field, even without network; every case is classified automatically, assigned to an agent and followed until the child has durably left work or risk.

    Reliable data

    A Data4Child form is evidence.

    Who collected it, where, when, on which phone: every form carries its guarantees, checked by the platform on receipt.

    Collection position

    Position recorded at the start and end of every form, compared with the agent's assigned area.

    Simulated positions detected

    Apps that fake the position are detected and the form is flagged.

    Signed by the phone

    Every form is signed with a key unique to the phone: it cannot be altered without the platform noticing.

    Registered phones

    The phone identifier (IMEI) is declared, then approved by the manager before any data is sent.

    Required fields

    An incomplete form is not sent: the answers used to classify the child are mandatory.

    One agent, their farmers

    Each agent sees only the farmers in their area; another agent can sign in only once the previous agent's forms are sent.

    Managing and assessing agents

    Set targets, follow the field, assess on facts.

    The CLMRS manager organises the season, assigns targets and measures each agent's performance from the forms actually collected.

    Assigned targets

    The season is split into dated phases. In each phase, the agent's target is to cover all of their farmers.

    Pace recalculated

    Every day, Data4Child works out the number of visits needed from what remains and the time available.

    Collection map

    The positions of each agent's forms appear on the map of their area: actual visits, routes, gaps.

    Performance reports

    Coverage, pace, forms to review, follow-up deadlines met: one report per agent, frozen at the end of each phase.

    Controlled extensions

    A phase ends on its date for the whole team; the manager can extend it for a specific agent.

    Two role levels

    The manager sees all statistics and all agents; each agent sees their own dashboard and farmers.

    How a season runs

    A complete method, from the community to the child.

    Data4Child follows the steps of a monitoring and remediation system: each step has its form, its agent and its deadline.

    1. 01

      Community profile

      A profile of each community, at the start of the season.

    2. 02

      Awareness

      Sessions in the localities assigned to each agent.

    3. 03

      Household survey

      Identification of working children or children at risk.

    4. 04

      Child interview

      Triggered by the survey, the child is registered with their household.

    5. 05

      Plantation visit

      Observation of working conditions on the plot.

    6. 06

      Remediation

      Support for the child, the household or the community.

    7. 07

      Follow-up

      Visits until exit, then a one-year check.

    Classification and deadlines

    The right action, at the right time.

    Every case is classified automatically by the core questionnaire rules. The level sets the pathway and the deadlines, which Data4Child monitors for you.

    • Immediate risk: emergency report and action within 24 hours
    • Confirmed child labour: awareness within 1 month, support within 6 months, follow-ups until two negative visits
    • Child at risk: awareness within 3 months, support within 1 year
    • Nothing to report: reassessment within 1 to 2 years
    • Support for the child, the household or the community, with proof of delivery

    Who runs the system

    The cooperative, or its exporter.

    One operator per cooperative, to avoid duplicate visits in the field.

    The cooperative runs it

    It takes the module and runs the season with its own agents. Its partner exporter always sees the overall statistics; named detail is shared only if the cooperative turns it on.

    The exporter runs it for its cooperatives

    When a cooperative has no system, the exporter deploys its own agents in its partner cooperatives and manages the whole programme.

    Data protection

    Children's data handled with the utmost care.

    Data4Child applies strict rules, on the phone as on the platform.

    Locked phones

    Encrypted local database, unlock code, remote wipe if lost or stolen.

    Authenticated forms

    Every form is signed by the registered phone that produced it, with its collection position.

    Restricted access

    Children's names are visible only to the CLMRS manager and the organisation's management.

    Nothing left behind

    Photos and signatures are removed from the phone as soon as their upload is confirmed.

    Data4Child builds on Sankora.

    Households, farmers and plots come from the Sankora register: nothing is re-keyed, and your EUDR compliance files can draw on your child labour monitoring.

    Frequently asked questions

    What we are asked most often.

    Do you need an internet connection in the field?
    No. The Android app works without network. Forms stay on the phone, encrypted, and are sent automatically as soon as a connection is available. The agent always sees what has been sent and what remains.
    Can the questionnaire be adapted?
    Yes. Each cooperative can add its own questions. The questions that identify children and their classification rules stay common, so results remain comparable.
    Who sees children's data?
    Named detail is restricted to the CLMRS manager and the management of the organisation running the system. Partners receive anonymous statistics, with small counts hidden.
    Which phones are needed?
    Android 8 or later. Each phone is registered, then approved by the manager before it receives any data.
    Does Data4Child work without Sankora?
    Data4Child is a Sankora module: it uses the register of farmers, households and plots. It is activated for a cooperative or an exporter, depending on who runs the system.
    How is Data4Child priced?
    As a module of the Sankora subscription, based on the number of farmers covered. The quote is prepared after the demo.
