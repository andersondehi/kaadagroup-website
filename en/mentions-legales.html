    • Data: the data you give us (usually your name, organisation, email address or phone number, and the content of your messages) and the number from which you call us or write to us on WhatsApp.
    • Recipients: the Kaada Group staff responsible for replying to you; our email service provider for emails.
    • Retention period: 12 months after our last exchange if your request does not lead to a contract. If it leads to a contract: for the whole duration of the contractual relationship, then for the periods required by law.

Kaada Group remains responsible for the exchanges it keeps, for the periods stated for contact requests. If you prefer, write to us at contact@kaadagroup.com or call us.

    • Legal basis: the steps prior to a possible employment contract, which you initiate by applying. Keeping your application for 24 months is based on your consent, which is optional and can be withdrawn at any time.

    • Recipients: the people involved in the recruitment and our email service provider.

With your written consent: 24 months after your application is sent, unless you withdraw that consent. If you are hired, your application becomes part of your employee file.
